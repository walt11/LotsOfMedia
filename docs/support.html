LotsOfMedia help

LotsOfMedia plays the movies, shows, music and Live TV on your own Plex or Jellyfin server on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Roku. The iPhone and iPad app also supports offline downloads, CarPlay and Siri.

Questions, problems or ideas — email:

LotsOfMedia@jwalter.net

Include your device model, operating system and app versions, your server type, and what happened. Do not send passwords, PINs, server tokens, or private media links.

What do I need?

Use a Plex account with access to a Plex Media Server, or connect to a Jellyfin server. For Jellyfin, enter the server address and sign in or use Quick Connect if your server supports it.

Is LotsOfMedia free?

Yes. All LotsOfMedia features available on your device are free to use. Live TV and DVR depend on your media server's setup. Plex and Jellyfin have their own account and server requirements.

Apple TV

Open LotsOfMedia on Apple TV and it shows a short code. On your phone or computer, go to plex.tv/link, sign in to Plex and enter the code. Plex Home profiles can switch inside the app. To use Jellyfin, choose the Jellyfin sign-in option. Put LotsOfMedia in the top row of the Home screen to see Continue Watching in the Top Shelf.

Switching Plex accounts or users

In Settings → Account, choose Switch Home User to change to another person in your Plex Home, or Sign In with Another Plex Account to sign out and sign in as someone else. Downloads stay on your device, and you can keep playing them offline from the sign-in screen.

A video won't play

Files iPhone can't play natively, like MKV, open in the built-in player. If you'd rather your server convert them, turn off Built-in Player for Other Formats in Settings → Playback Preferences. While AirPlaying, your server converts them automatically.

Downloads

Downloads continue in the background and resume after interruptions. Choose quality, Wi-Fi-only and storage limits in Settings. A download at a smaller quality is converted by your server first.

CarPlay and Siri

LotsOfMedia appears in CarPlay automatically. To use Siri, tap Use Siri to Play Music in Settings, then say "Play playlist name in LotsOfMedia".

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no LotsOfMedia server, no analytics, no tracking.
