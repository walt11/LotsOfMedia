Privacy Policy — Effective November 27, 2026

How LotsOfMedia handles your data

LotsOfMedia is an app for watching and listening to media on your own Plex or Jellyfin server. The app connects to Plex services, the media servers you choose, and Apple services used by optional features such as Siri. There is no LotsOfMedia server, app analytics, advertising, or in-app tracking.

§01

Plex sign-in & your servers

To use LotsOfMedia you sign in with your Plex account, through Plex's own sign-in page, or enter a server's address yourself. The app then works directly with:

  • plex.tv — to sign you in, list the servers your account can reach, show and switch between Plex Home users (including the PIN you type for a protected user), and load profile pictures and some artwork.
  • Your Plex Media Server — to browse your libraries, stream and download media, and keep it informed as you use the app: what you play and how far you get (so Continue Watching and watched status stay in sync), playlists you create or edit (including a cover photo you choose for one), Live TV recordings you schedule, and subtitles you search for (your server looks those up with its own subtitle providers).

If you connect to Jellyfin, your username and password go to the Jellyfin server you enter for sign-in, or you can use Quick Connect. Your selected server receives requests for library content, playback, and supported library changes. Sign-in tokens are stored on your device. Requests can include app and device information such as the version and an installation identifier so the service can recognize the client.

§02

What stays on your device

  • Downloads — movies, episodes, songs and their artwork and subtitles, so they play offline.
  • Offline progress — how far you got while offline, sent to your server the next time you're connected.
  • A copy of library pages you've browsed, so they still open without a connection.
  • Your settings and preferences — theme, app icon, playback and download choices, Favorites, Watch Later, recent searches, your listening queue, and when you last played each playlist. Favorites and Watch Later are kept on this device and aren't sent anywhere.
  • On Apple TV, your Continue Watching titles and their artwork links, so the Top Shelf can show them. They're kept on the Apple TV, separately for each Apple TV user, and removed when you sign out.
§03

What we don't collect

  • No LotsOfMedia account and no LotsOfMedia server.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or usage-tracking code of any kind.
  • No advertising identifiers, ad networks, or cross-app tracking.
  • No location, contacts, or health data.
§04

Apple features

  • Siri — if you allow it, you can say "Play <playlist, artist, album or song> in LotsOfMedia". Your voice is handled by Apple. The app gives Siri the names of your music playlists so it can recognize them.
  • CarPlay, AirPlay, the Lock Screen and the Dynamic Island show what's playing and download progress, using Apple's standard system features on your devices.

These are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

§06

The built-in player

Files iPhone can't play natively (such as MKV) play in a built-in player based on VLCKit, an open-source library by VideoLAN. It runs entirely on your device and sends nothing anywhere except requests for the video to your own server.

§07

Your controls

  • Remove downloads one at a time, or all at once in Settings.
  • Sign out in Settings, which removes your Plex sign-in from this device.
  • Removing the app removes its app container and downloads. Keychain items and files you exported may persist. Sign out before removing the app to remove the associated sign-in.
  • What your Plex server keeps (such as watch history) is managed in Plex, under your Plex account.
§08

Security

Connections to plex.tv use HTTPS. Connections to your server use HTTPS when your server offers it, which Plex servers normally do. Sign-in tokens are saved in the device's Keychain on Apple platforms or in the channel's local registry on Roku.

§09

How this app was built

LotsOfMedia was built with the help of AI. AI coding assistants were used during the creation of the app. LotsOfMedia does not send your media or library information to those assistants.

§10

Children's privacy

LotsOfMedia is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not automatically send personal information to the developer.
